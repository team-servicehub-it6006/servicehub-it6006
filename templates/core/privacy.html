Privacy statement

Fresh Start Home Cleaning collects some information about you so that we can clean your house and send you a bill. This page says what we collect, who sees it, and what you can ask us to do about it.

What we collect

Your name, email address and mobile number. The address we are cleaning, how many bedrooms and bathrooms it has, and any notes you give us about getting in. Your booking history with us. We do not take card details on this website.

Why we collect it

To make the booking, to tell the right cleaner where to go, and to contact you if something changes. That is all. We do not use it for marketing and we do not sell it to anybody.

Who can see it

You can see everything we hold about you by signing in. The cleaner assigned to your job can see the address and your access notes, and can see your phone number on the day of the job. Our office staff can see your bookings. Nobody else can.

How long we keep it

Booking records are kept for seven years, because we have to. Your access notes are deleted thirty days after the job. If you have not booked with us for two years we will email you, and then remove your personal details from our system.

How we look after it

The site runs over an encrypted connection. Your password is stored in a scrambled form that nobody here can read, so we will never ask you for it. Our staff only see the jobs they are working on, and we keep a record of who changes what.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, and you can ask us to correct it. You can change your own contact details when you are signed in. Email us and we will reply within twenty working days. If you are not happy with how we have handled it, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz.

Where your information is kept

On servers in New Zealand.
