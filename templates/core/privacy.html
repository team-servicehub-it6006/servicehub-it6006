Privacy statement

ServiceHub stores the contact and booking details needed to arrange a home clean. Do not enter real customer information in the demonstration accounts.

Information collected

Accounts contain a name, email, phone number and suburb. Bookings contain the service, date, time, property address, property size and optional access notes. The website does not collect payment card details.

Who has access

Customers can view and edit their own details and bookings. Assigned cleaners can see their jobs, including the address and access notes. Administrators manage bookings, services, accounts and roles. Access checks are applied on the server.

Retention

The maintenance command removes access notes thirty days after recorded completion or cancellation. The operator must schedule this command for regular cleanup. Booking and account records remain until the operator follows an approved retention process; inactive accounts are not automatically deleted.

Security and location

Passwords are hashed. Sign-in attempts are limited, forms use request verification, and role changes and assignments are logged. The local demonstration stores its database on the computer running it and uses a local HTTP connection. A public deployment needs HTTPS, email configuration and an approved hosting location. New Zealand hosting has not been established by this package.

Access and correction

Sign in to update your profile and review your bookings. Contact the operator for a full data copy, further corrections or deletion requests. No automated data export or account deletion is provided.
